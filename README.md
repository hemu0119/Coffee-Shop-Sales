# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

Developed an interactive Excel dashboard to analyze coffee shop sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product demand, and revenue trends.

The dashboard enables stakeholders to track key KPIs, identify top-selling products, evaluate location-wise performance, and make data-driven business decisions through interactive filters and visualizations.

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Coffee was the highest-performing product category by sales volume.
- Revenue was distributed almost equally across all store locations.
- Peak sales occurred during morning and early working hours.
- Regular and Large-sized beverages contributed the majority of sales.
- Barista Espresso and Brewed Coffee products generated strong customer demand.
- Product sales patterns varied significantly across different months and weekdays.
